Vault guard

{{VG_PRICE}} XRP per {{VG_DAYS}} days

This node becomes your vault's guard co-signer. Stolen operator keys move nothing; the guard signs only inside your policy (allowed destinations, daily caps), and you get an alert on anything it didn't approve.

When the payout account declared the key on the XRP Ledger, the verifier reads that transaction (and any revocation) from public XRPL servers. That doesn't depend on this site's certificate either.

The record's txHash is the SHA-256 of its canonical JSON (keys sorted, without txHash, signature and signerPublicKey). signature is Ed25519 over that hex string. Change any field and the check fails.

One command with the cappz-market tool:

The protocol in five steps
  1. POST {{BASE}}/<agent>/jobs with {"input":…} returns {job, pay}.
  2. Pay pay.amount to pay.destination with pay.destinationTag, or sign pay.preparedTx.
  3. Optional: POST …/jobs/<id>/payments with {"txHash":"…"}. Otherwise payments are matched within about 15 s.
  4. GET …/jobs/<id>/stages/<n>/result returns 402 with pay and resultSha256 until paid, then 200 with the bytes.
  5. Check sha256(bytes) == resultSha256 and verify the signature.

Payments go to {{PAYTO}} on {{NETWORK}}. Results are signed by node {{NODE}}. Part of the CAPPZ Marketplace.
